## Live Gameplay Tooling (future capabilities)

- **[ ]** **Unrealized/at-risk credit warning.** Surfaced directly by the Session Spotlights page: its #1 most-lucrative session read at first glance as an outlier "incredible" combat session, but was actually a typical combat run into which a substantial amount of *already-earned but not-yet-cashed-in* exobiology credits had been carried — an unrecognized risk, since defeat in that combat would have lost those unrealized proceeds entirely. Proposed as a live-gameplay capability (not the batch report tool): forewarn the player when they're carrying a substantial amount of unrealized/uncashed value (bounty vouchers, combat bonds, exploration/exobiology sale data not yet turned in) while engaged in something risky, so it can be cashed in deliberately rather than lost to bad luck. Two distinct motivations to account for, not just one: (1) plain financial prudence — don't carry a fortune into a fight; and (2) BGS-effect intentionality — turning in bounties/bonds is sometimes specifically meant to influence faction standing (see the Colonisation & BGS section below), so a reminder to deposit regularly also protects against *missing that goal* through unexpected loss, not just against the credit loss itself. Consistent with `design-principles.md`'s recommendation-format pattern (a forewarning/recommendation the player acts on, not an automatic cash-in) — not yet scoped as its own feature doc; a candidate for promotion once picked up, per `index.md`'s "not yet written up" list.

## Exploration

- **[x] Exploration destination hypothesis (star type as a pre-travel filter) — analyzed and written up.** Full re-derivation, superseding the earlier never-written-up offer: high-value planets (Earthlike/Water/Ammonia/Water giant/life-bearing gas giants), pristine rings (especially pristine metallic), and biological finds were all cross-referenced against each system's primary/arrival star type (reconstructed via the same ASOF-join pattern used for ship/vehicle-state attribution elsewhere in this project, since most `Scan`/`ScanOrganic`/`CodexEntry` events don't carry `StarSystem` directly). Consistent pattern across all three categories: A/F/G/K-class primary stars hit at 2-4x the rate of M dwarfs (7.0% vs. 20-24% for high-value planets, similarly for rings and bio) and at 10x-to-near-zero versus brown dwarfs/T Tauri stars. Pristine metallic rings skew even further toward hot/massive stars (O/B/F) and co-occur with a high-value planet 60% of the time. Bio finds are never on the Earthlike/Water/Ammonia world itself — always on a plain Rocky/HMC/Icy/Rocky-Ice body in the same system. Full methodology, tables, and caveats (selection-bias risk chief among them) in the new `exploration-destination-hypothesis.md`.
